Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Levelwell is run by William Kelsall, trading as Levelwell ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. We never sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

What we collect

Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password.

Information you enter: your first name, the medication you were on, your treatment and last-dose dates, your weights, protein and strength logs, food-noise check-ins, reading progress and app settings. Some of this is health information.

Meal photos you choose to analyse: sent securely to our AI provider to estimate protein, then discarded. We don’t keep the photo.

Basic technical data needed to run the service, such as error logs and the number of photo estimates you’ve used today.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

To provide the app and back up your progress (contract).

Health information is processed only with your explicit consent, which you give when you create an account (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

To keep the service secure and prevent misuse, such as limiting photo estimates (legitimate interests).

Our website and waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we collect only your email address, to tell you when Levelwell launches. We don’t use it for anything else and we don’t share it. You can ask us to remove it at any time by emailing us. The website itself doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who processes it for us

Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions, hosted in the EU, Ireland).

Anthropic (AI meal-photo estimates; photos are not stored or used to train models under our agreement).

Apple and RevenueCat (subscriptions and payments; we never see your card details).

Resend (sending sign-in and password emails).

Each provider acts under a data processing agreement. Where data leaves the UK, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete my account), we delete your account and everything in it straight away. Backups roll off within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email us.

Contact: (enable JavaScript to see our email address). If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Levelwell is for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Levelwell is a habits and tracking tool, not medical advice. Talk to your prescriber about stopping or changing your medication.
